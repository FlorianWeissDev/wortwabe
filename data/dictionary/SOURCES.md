# Dictionary sources

`words.json` is generated by `tools/build-dictionary.ts`. Do not edit it by hand — edit
`allowlist.txt` / `blocklist.txt` and regenerate.

## Source

| Asset | Origin | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Word list, part-of-speech tags, frequency tiers | [`open-crossword-bank`](https://github.com/tamino-martinius/node-open-crossword-bank), German enriched subset | Data **CC0-1.0**, code MIT |
| Frequency ranking upstream of that package | [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) | MIT |

CC0 data carries no attribution obligation and no share-alike clause, so the generated
dictionary and puzzle files can be committed and shipped without conditions. This is why the
package was chosen over hunspell `de_DE` (GPL) and over corpus frequency lists such as DWDS or
Leipzig, which are partly non-commercial or share-alike.

## What the build keeps

Only the ~18k **enriched** entries are used, because they are the ones carrying part-of-speech
tags. Entries are kept when they are tagged `noun`, `verb`, `adjective` or `adverb`, are at least
four letters, and contain nothing but a–z.

The source's `other` bucket is dropped wholesale — that is where proper nouns, abbreviations and
foreign fragments collect (GOETHE, WIKIPEDIA, BEARB, TOGETHER). Dropping it is the single most
effective quality filter available, and the reason the base 90k list is not used at all.

## Known limitations

- **Proper nouns still leak at roughly 4 %.** A 50-word spot check of the generated dictionary
  found two (*Julius*, *Griechenlands*): given names and geographic genitives that the source
  tags as ordinary nouns. They are removed via `blocklist.txt` as they surface during puzzle
  review. There is no signal in the data that would catch them automatically.
- **Inflected forms appear standalone** (*Reichs*, *Worten*, *Museums*). Valid German, slightly
  odd in isolation, harmless in play.
- **Naturalized loanwords are kept** (*Couch*, *Account*, *Atelier*, *Western*). Deliberate — they
  are ordinary German usage.
- Umlauts and ß are excluded by the game's rules, which removes a meaningful slice of German
  vocabulary. See `plans/03-game-rules.md`.
